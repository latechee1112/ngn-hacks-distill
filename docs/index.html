Distill

Simplifies cluttered webpages based on your calibrated visual preferences.

Scan to open this page on your phone

Or just keep reading — everything below works from this tab too. The code points at whatever URL this page is loaded from.

1. Install (about 30 seconds)

  1. Download the extension: extension.zip, then unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Distill opens a one-time calibration tab — click Continue without camera if you'd rather skip straight to trying it (camera calibration personalizes results, but isn't required).

2. Try it on a sample page

Open the demo article below — it's deliberately cluttered with ads, sidebars, and a newsletter popup, the kind of page Distill is built for. Click the Distill icon in your toolbar to simplify it.
